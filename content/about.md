**PHP developer · Symfony · Team lead** · Craiova, Romania (working remotely)

I have been building web applications with PHP for 20 years, mostly with Symfony, which I have used in every major version from 1 to 6. My work has covered telecom, large e-commerce and marketplace platforms, and I have spent more than ten of those years in lead roles while still writing code.
